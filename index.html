Homebuying Guide - 6 Months

3 Rules for HomeBuying

  1. Plan to hold for 5 years or more
    • Why: When home prices have dipped, that dip has been roughly about 5 years. That way you don’t lose money.
  2. Be comfortable in your budget
  3. 3 months expenses saved

Planning Phase (3 Months)

Day 1 - Write down your "Why"

  • Write it down on paper with your partner
  • Remind yourself why you’re actually purchasing
  • Don’t get swept up in FOMO
  • Without a why, budget creep will be high!
  • Examples: Want to be done with renting, Relocating because of Job or Family, Need more space due to a growing family, Need an office due to transitioning to WFH, Health reasons, More privacy, Schooldistricts that are preferred etc

Day 2 - Budget Clean-Up

  • Consider a budgeting software (what’s coming in and what’s going out on a monthly basis)
  • Trick to find a comfortable payment without a budget
  • What is and isn’t working?
    • Is there a leak in your budget?
    • Are you actually able to save?
  • Debt assessment - is now a good time to make a large purchase?

Day 3 - Guard Your Headspace

  • Consider where you want to get home buying news from, don’t let it just come to you
  • Unsubscribe from channels, block accounts etc
  • Stick to your goals and only rely on news to actually gather info you’re seeking
    • Most news is out to making you afraid and fearful, so don’t get swept away at that

Day 5 - 15 - Polish Your Credit

  • Doing this early will give you time to get your best score as quickly as possible

Day 20 - Gather Your Documents

  • ID (DL, Passport)
  • Past 2 years:
    • Tax Returns
    • W2s/1099s
  • Recently monthly bank statements (or other accounts for down payment)
  • Most recent paystub (if applicable)
  • Tip: Put it in Google Drive (share to loan officer)

Day 22 - Prevent Spam Calls / Texts

  • (Trigger Leads) Credit Unions sell your information to other lenders - Can go to upto 50 or 70 calls a day!!
  • OptOutPrescreen.com
  • DoNotCall.gov

Day 25 - Talk With a Loan Officer

  • Schedule a call with us (Real Estate Agent - winthehouseyoulove)
    • Licensed in all 50 states
    • Consultation Call to find the best loan that makes sense for you
    • 1 application, one credit pull, 70 lenders
    • Federally Charted Bank through Allied First Bank
  • Verify what you can get approved for (before the loan officer)
  • See your rate, monthly payment, price range and est. out of pocket expenses

Day 27 - Start Practice Payments (Stress Testing)

  • Act like you already have your mortgage payment (if higher)
  • Save the difference of your rent and est. mortgage payment into a savings account and don’t touch it
  • See if the payment creates tension in your budget

Day 28 - Create Your Savings Plan

  • Practice Payments
  • Example:
    • Needed - Saved = Remaining
    • $18,000 (Needed) - $8,000 (Saved) = $10,000 (Remaining)
    • $10,000 (Remaining) / 5 Months = $2,000/month (Savings Needed)

Day 30 - Dream, seriously

  • We need to do the full home buying dream thing so we can get to the realistic stuff without the baggage
  • Go to open houses for fun
  • Scroll, scroll, scroll (on Zillow)
  • Screenshot and make a folder of houses you love
  • Get it out of your system, feel through it
  • Get it out of your system and come back to what is practically feasible

Day 35 - Decide What You Want

  • Neighborhood research
    • Walking
    • Driving by at different times of day
    • Talking to people in the community
    • Safety, School Districts, Proximity to work
  • Open Houses
  • Create Needs / Wants List
    • Needs need to accomplish why you’re buying the house. Those are not negotiable
    • Wants can be compromised on. How to solve for non-needs so that everyone is happy?
    • What will you compromise on?
    • Allow room to grow and expand - Sometimes first homes aren’t what you always want. Second homes and third homes aligns more and more with what you want

Day 40 - Plan Your Exit

  • How will you get out of your lease?
    • Make remaining payments
    • Negotiate a buyout
    • Have someone take over the lease
  • Will you sell or rent?
    • Do you need money from the sale?
    • Can you afford 2 mortgage payments?
    • Will your offer be contingent?
  • Create a fallback plan if buying doesn’t work out

Day 55 - Get A Home Inspector

  • You won’t need them until you’re under contract on a home
  • Get recommendations from your real estate agent
  • Explore online reviews

Day 60 - Organize Moving Logistics

  • Moving company - get quotes if needed
  • Recruit friends early and honestly (don’t rope in the pickup truck guy)
  • Explore local truck rental options if needed
  • (When ready) Begin packing non-essentials early - do it in stages instead of the one TERRIBLE weekend

Shopping Phase (2 Months)

Explore Homes

  • Talk with your agent about your entire situation
    • Ask them “how realistic is this?”
    • “What might I compromise on?”
  • Realistically look at homes online
  • Visit at least 3 open houses
  • Walk neighborhoods you’re looking in and narrow the pockets you want to buy in
  • Schedule showings of homes you like

Review Comps (Comparable Homes)

  • Once you found a house you’re super interested!
  • Have your agent “pull comps” to validate the value & structure an offer
  • Schedule showing with agent to see the home
  • (You’re ready to take action at this stage because of all the prep)

Make An Offer

  • When you’re ready!
  • This is called being “under contract”
